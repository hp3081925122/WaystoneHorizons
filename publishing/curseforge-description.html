Waystone Horizons

A Waystones addon that adds three reusable warp stones with configurable cooldowns and lower teleport experience costs.

  • Diamond Warp Stone: 150-second default cooldown and a 1-level experience discount.
  • Netherite Warp Stone: 60-second default cooldown and a 2-level experience discount.
  • Nether Star Warp Stone: 30-second default cooldown and a 3-level experience discount.

Experience costs never fall below zero. Point-based costs are discounted using the experience value of the corresponding levels. Cooldowns can be changed in the server configuration and are shared between the three stones. The original Waystones charge-up, damage interruption, particles, destination selection and durability behavior are retained.

Diamond and Nether Star Warp Stones use four amethyst shards in the corners, four ender pearls on the edges, and the corresponding material in the center. Upgrade a Diamond Warp Stone in a smithing table using a Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template and a Netherite Ingot.

Requires Waystones and Balm. This is an independent addon, not an official Waystones release. Original Waystones artwork belongs to its respective author.

中文介绍

跃石远境是 Waystones 的附属模组，添加三种可重复使用的传送石：

  • 钻石传送石：默认冷却 150 秒，传送经验费用减少 1 级。
  • 下界合金传送石：默认冷却 60 秒，传送经验费用减少 2 级。
  • 下界之星传送石：默认冷却 30 秒，传送经验费用减少 3 级。

经验费用最低为 0，不会因减免反向增加经验。经验点收费按对应等级的经验价值折算减免。三项冷却均可在服务端配置中修改，三种传送石共享冷却组。保留原模组的蓄力、受伤打断、粒子、传送目标界面与耐久机制。

钻石与下界之星传送石均由四角紫水晶碎片、四边末影珍珠以及中心对应材料合成。下界合金传送石使用下界合金升级锻造模板、钻石传送石与下界合金锭在锻造台升级。

需要安装 Waystones 和 Balm。本项目是独立附属，并非 Waystones 官方版本。原 Waystones 图案归原作者所有。
